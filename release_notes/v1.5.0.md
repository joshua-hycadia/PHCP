PHCP 1.5.0 - Perl Hydraulic Calculation Program

A Hardy Cross loop-balancing hydraulic solver for fire sprinkler and hydrant systems: a plain-text command language for the model (`.phc`), three windows on that one engine, a calculation report in ASCII text or PDF, an AutoCAD exporter and a pyRevit extension. Australian units by default (L/min, kPa, m, mm).

## Downloads

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `phcp_editor.exe` | the text editor: write the model, VALIDATE (F4), CALCULATE (F5), WRITE REPORT (Ctrl+R) |
| `phcp_sheet.exe` | the spreadsheet: the same `.phc` file and the same engine, entered as a sheet |
| `phcp_isometric.exe` | the same calculation, drawn on an isometric grid |
| `phcp.exe` | the command line: `phcp.exe model.phc report.pdf`, `phcp.exe --save model.phc` to keep the results in the model, or `phcp.exe --json model.phc` for another program |

All four are self-contained Windows programs with the pipe and fitting database and the report typeface packed inside. They replace the 1.4.0 files in place.

## What changed since 1.4.0

- **Estimated dry pipe valve trip, transit and delivery times** (report Section 13, the JSON's `dry_pipe_valves[].estimate`, and `DEVICE` records in the results block). They are ESTIMATES: they change no other figure, decide no acceptance, and are not a water delivery time calculation by a listed method.
  - **Trip time** by the FM Global formula published in the SFPE Handbook, t = 0.0352 V / (An sqrt T0) ln(pa0 / pa): V the dry volume downstream of the valve, An the open orifice area, T0 the air temperature, pa0 the set air pressure (`DPVAIR`) and pa the trip pressure (the valve's TRIP, or the static water pressure divided by its RATIO).
  - **Transit time**, rudimentary and ignoring the air: the larger of the water front's travel time to the most remote outlet (the sum of L / v along the flow path) and the time to fill the dry volume, at the demand point's flows, or else the operating point's. A lower bound.
  - **Delivery time**, trip plus transit.
- **New command `DPVTRIP <PIPE> <ORIFICE> [COUNT] [AIRTEMP] [STATIC]`**: the open orifice, how many are open (1 if not given), the air temperature (20 C if not given) and the static water pressure at the valve for a RATIO trip (if not given, the highest supply's pressure at no flow, corrected for elevation).
- **New checks**: a set air pressure not above the trip pressure REJECTS the calculation (the valve cannot be set); a trip pressure below 1.893 atmospheres absolute, where the orifice is no longer choked, is a warning; a dry part that reaches a water supply is refused.
- No steady-state result changed: every example solves to the same flows and pressures as in 1.4.0. JSON interface 2 is unchanged; `estimate` is a new field.

## Planned

- **Accelerators**: their data in the database and their effect on trip time. An accelerator given as ON is not modelled, and the report says so.
- **The air in the transit**: the water as a plug pushing the dry pipework's air out through the open orifice, so the transit is not only a lower bound.

Neither is started.
