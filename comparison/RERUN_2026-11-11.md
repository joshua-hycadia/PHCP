# Re-run, 11 November 2026: PHCP on main against the other programs

PHCP on `main` at 6a24a51 (1.5.0 with the supply check valves, the audit's
false-positive refusals and the independent benchmarks) re-solved all 900
models of round 1 and round 2 on GitHub's Linux runners, by the method of the
original run: the demand point, then the network with the supply at it
(`tools/rerun_phcp.py`, the `comparison` workflow).  The other programs were
not run again: their results are what they gave on 4 November 2026, and only
PHCP has changed since.

## Result

**All 900 solved, and nothing moved.**  The largest change in any required
supply pressure from PHCP 1.0.0 is 0.004% (0.026 kPa); in any head pressure
0.005 kPa; in any pipe flow 0.08 L/min.  Those are the solvers' own
tolerances.  So the agreement with the other programs is exactly that of
4 November:

|---|---|---|---|
| AU Calc Program | +0.74% (1.40%) | +0.77% (1.41%) | +0.67% (5.29%) |
| US Calc Program 1 | +0.18% (1.05%) | +0.15% (0.96%) | not run |
| US Calc Program 2 | -0.02% (0.04%) | -0.02% (0.04%) | not run |
| US Calc Program 3 | -0.01% (0.04%) | -0.01% (0.04%) | not run |
| EPANET 2.2 | +0.48% (1.03%) | +0.50% (1.03%) | +0.35% (1.95%) |

The explanations in [README.md](README.md) and [ROUND2.md](ROUND2.md) stand:
the two programs reading higher use the 1.852 / 4.871 form of Hazen-Williams.

## What the re-run found: a slowdown, now fixed

Rounds 1 and A ran in about the time they took on 1.0.0 (median x0.82 and
x0.93; a different computer).  **Suite B ran 26 times slower** (median), up
to 102 times:

| Suite B, PHCP run time | 1.0.0 | main at 6a24a51 |
|---|---|---|
| trees, median (max) | 3.9 s (65 s) | 130 s (1146 s) |
| loops | 4.8 s (84 s) | 181 s (1592 s) |
| grids | 35 s (3089 s) | 359 s (5359 s) |

The cause was the demand search's floor trial, added on 11 November to fix a
gravity tank's demand point (PR 48).  It tried a 0 kPa supply first, on every
model.  Behind a backflow preventer and a pressure reducing valve a 0 kPa
supply cannot drive the network at all, and that trial ran the Hardy Cross
iteration to its limit before failing: 5 to 8 s on a small tree, minutes on a
grid, twice per model.  The floor is now tried only when every other trial
passed down to the search's resolution, which is the only case it can
matter.  `tools/test_check_valves.pl` holds both: the gravity tank still met
at 0 kPa, and no 0 kPa trial on a model with a BFP and a PRV.  On T047 the
run went from 6.2 s to 0.4 s, with the same requirement to 0.0001 kPa.

## Acceptance at the requirement

Each model's second solve, with the supply exactly at its requirement
(rounded to 0.0001 kPa), is ACCEPTED for 185 models of round 1 and suite A
and 173 of suite B, and NONCOMPLIANT for the rest.  That is the harness, not
PHCP: at the requirement the governing head sits on its minimum, and the
rounding leaves it short in the last digit (T079: 56.57 L/min against 56.57
required; 0.001 kPa more and it is ACCEPTED).
